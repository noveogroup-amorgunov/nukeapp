# Product

What the system supports now. History lives in `changes/archive/`.

## Catalog

- Product catalog browsing
- Categories
- Category sorting via shareable `sortBy` URLs, preserved on reload and browser
  back/forward; available when the `productsSort` feature flag is enabled
- Popular products
- Product details
- Stock availability: "Out of stock" state on catalog card and
  product page, "Only 1 left" badge when a single unit remains

## Cart

- Add / remove products
- Quantity management
- Quantity is limited by product Stock

## Wishlist

- Add / remove products from wishlist

## Session

- Authorization (Login / Logout)

## Preferences

- Theme switching
- Feature flags
